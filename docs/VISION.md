# Product Vision

## Mission

Road to 12% should make consistent, safe, and measurable training feel as clear as following directions from a trusted personal coach. The app should reduce planning friction while preserving user control, workout history, and the intent of the training program.

## Long-term experience

### AI personal coach

The long-term coach should interpret workout completion, recovery, readiness, available equipment, and user feedback to offer understandable recommendations. Recommendations must explain why an action is suggested, estimate its impact, and require user confirmation before changing the plan.

### Adaptive workout scheduling

Scheduling should respond to missed workouts, travel, illness, recovery needs, and changing availability without losing the program sequence. Planned dates remain the historical intent; scheduled dates may move. Rest days and explicit recovery constraints remain protected.

### Progressive overload

The app should use completed sets, repetitions, selected weight, effort ratings, and recent recovery to recommend safe progression. Recommendations should be conservative, traceable, and easy to accept or override.

### Exercise education

Every exercise should provide equipment-specific setup, execution steps, coaching cues, common mistakes, target muscles, and appropriately licensed visual guidance. Education should remain available before and during a workout.

### Nutrition

Future nutrition support should connect energy intake, protein, hydration, and weight trends to the training goal without becoming a prescriptive medical system. Nutrition guidance should emphasize sustainable habits and clearly state its limitations.

### Progress tracking

Progress should combine program adherence, total sessions, training performance, body measurements, recovery signals, and personal records. The product should favor meaningful trends over pressure-inducing vanity metrics.

### Offline-first design

Core workouts, exercise guidance, scheduling, and history must continue to work without a network connection. Network-backed capabilities should degrade gracefully and never block access to saved training data.

### PWA support

The app should feel native when installed on an iPhone Home Screen: fast launch, safe-area-aware layout, stable navigation, touch-friendly controls, resilient local state, and reliable Service Worker updates.

## Product principles

1. Preserve user data and training intent.
2. Explain recommendations before applying them.
3. Keep the workout usable with one hand on a small phone.
4. Make offline behavior a core requirement, not a fallback.
5. Prefer incremental, backward-compatible evolution.
6. Keep exercise instruction accurate, licensed, and equipment-specific.
